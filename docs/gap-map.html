Read: the vertical axis is "leverage of the knob" — how many times the tail improves from the worse to the better setting of that knob at fixed everything else. The placement bar collapses from 22× to ~1× exactly when the workload becomes production-bursty, while the order bar persists (27×→13×) and absorption (width) appears as a 2× completion lever.

4 · The theory triangle — three edges held, one corner open

Three mature literatures each hold one edge of the triangle; every one holds the other two edges fixed.

Edge 2's state is queue length, not job history — that distinction is what keeps the corner open. Our M1 (interleaving) is a decreasing C(j); M3 is the history law; M4 prices migration. A restricted model (two cache states per machine, unit jobs) plus the Ayesta-Righter release-time equivalence is the entry point for a theorem.
