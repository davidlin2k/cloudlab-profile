# DR-010: The two decisive experiments, in order

**Date:** September 26, 2026

We are one causal test away from knowing whether this is a paper. Stop all other threads of work. Run RQ1, then RQ3. Nothing else until those two answers are in.

## Framing (why only these two)

The paper's whole case rests on two facts we do not yet have:
1. **RQ1 — causality:** the placement-correction path (stop-and-rearm off-CPU), not merely the off-CPU location, is what strands work.
2. **RQ3 — significance:** it triggers under realistic bursts, not only a synthetic 790k-pps flood.

If RQ1 fails, there is no paper on this mechanism. If RQ1 holds but RQ3 shows only an extreme single-queue flood triggers it, the paper is small. Both are cheap to answer. Everything else (the driver audit, the repair design space, the second-driver runs, the theory) is worth building only after these two land.

## Experiment 1 — RQ1, causality (run first)

**Freeze `specs/p1-CAUSAL.md` before any run.**

The confound in E2 was that pinning the thread to CPU 8 changed *both* the execution location *and* whether the completion path fires. This experiment holds location fixed and varies only the mechanism.

| | Arm A (driver as-is) | Arm B (keep-polling) |
|---|---|---|
| Thread affinity | Pinned to CPU 10 (off-mask, fixed) | Pinned to CPU 10 (off-mask, fixed) |
| IRQ 312 affinity | CPU 8 (fixed) | CPU 8 (fixed) |
| Budget-exhausted off-mask poll | complete + rearm (unmodified) | keep polling (the F1 patch) |
| Load | 790k flood, then reduce to 158k, then probe | same |
| Cells | 8 | 8 |

**Both arms pin the thread to the same wrong CPU.** The only difference is the F1 patch. That isolates the mechanism from the location.

**The readiness probe is the primary measurement and must be trustworthy.** During every gap where `rx7_packets` is flat, sample, at 1 kHz, whether the CQE at the consumer index is software-owned (ready but unserved). Use the owner-parity bit at the consumer index; do not rely on the earlier occupancy walk that had the unlocated-consumer caveat. Log, per gap: duration, whether a ready CQE was present throughout, CPU, and `ch7_events`/`ch7_arm` deltas.

**Pre-registered predictions:**
- Arm A: stalls reproduce, and during the long gaps a ready CQE is present but unserved (work stranded on an idle-enough CPU).
- Arm B: on the same CPU 10, service continues, gaps shrink, stranded-work events go to zero.

**Decision:**
- **A strands ready work AND B restores service on the same CPU → causality confirmed.** Proceed to RQ3.
- **B still stalls, OR A never shows a ready CQE during gaps → the mechanism is not the cause.** Stop. Write it up as a negative result, keep the placement measurements, and open a separate investigation into their cause. Do not add theory to rescue the hypothesis.

**Deliverable:** `notes/p1-CAUSAL-1.md` — per-arm stall counts, the stranded-work evidence per gap, counts only, no interpretation. Bring it to me before drawing conclusions.

## Experiment 2 — RQ3, significance (only if RQ1 confirms)

**Freeze `specs/p1-TRIGGER.md` first.**

Find the smallest realistic condition that triggers the stall, in the default and documented configurations.

- **Configurations:** (a) default — threaded on, unpinned; (b) the documented busy-poll setup — thread pinned to an unused core.
- **Workload:** replace the raw flood with bursts. Sweep burst rate (100k–790k pps) and burst duration (10 ms–5 s), with idle gaps between. Also run one real service: a UDP echo or DNS responder under incast from the 5 senders.
- **Metric:** does a stall occur, and the smallest (rate × duration) that triggers one.

**Pre-registered thresholds:**
- **Strong significance:** stalls at bursts at or below a single 100 Gb/s sender's realistic output, in the default config.
- **Weak significance:** only sustained near-line-rate single-queue floods trigger it.

**Decision:**
- **Strong → full paper.** Proceed to build the driver audit, the second-driver runs (i40e, mlx4), and the repair evaluation.
- **Weak → narrow paper** (the contract and the lesson, mlx5 case study, fix), or workshop. Reassess venue with me.

**Deliverable:** `notes/p1-TRIGGER-1.md` — the trigger envelope, and whether a real service stalls.

## Why this and nothing else

We have spent recent effort sharpening the question and correcting evidence, not adding confirmation. These two experiments are the confirmation. RQ1 tells us if the mechanism is real; RQ3 tells us if it matters. Two answers, a few days, and then we know what we're writing — or that we're not. Resist every temptation to build the general machinery before these land.
