## The per-gap device state (the frozen table's inputs)

EVERY analyzed gap -- all 138, including every event-silent one --
shows the same state:

- **eq_ci frozen** across the gap (a single value; per-gap check).
- **eq_devw = 0 for all 4 slots** (no DEVICE-WRITTEN EQE at the
  frozen cons_index: **the EQ is empty** -- the device wrote
  NOTHING into the completion EQ, in any gap, at any sample).
- **I_in = 0**: zero hardware IRQ entries on the vector (tracepoint
  irq:irq_handler_entry, filtered to the queue's irq 312) inside
  any gap.
- **E_in = 0**: zero completion events inside any gap.
- The IRQ affinity is constant (8) at every 1 Hz sample; no
  affinity change at any gap onset.
- rx_out_of_buffer CLIMBS during the long silences (T1A-1:
  661682421 -> 721093202 through the 279 s silence; T1A-2:
  736170511 -> 755520528) -- packets are ARRIVING and being
  discarded while the queue sits unserved (the memo's expected
  consequence; logged, not classified on).
- The CQ is ARMED at gap time: clean parity (adb_sn == arm_sn & 3)
  in 1982/1983 samples of a sampled mid-wedge window (T1A-1
  300-302 s; the 1 off-sample is the between-gap transient) --
  consistent with the DR-012 step-2 result (clean 67/67).

## The branch call (the frozen table, verbatim)

"EQ empty and armed, CQ armed, completions pending" ->
**Device/firmware. The vendor-bug-report branch.**

Branch 1 (interrupt delivery) is EXCLUDED: there are no EQEs for
an interrupt to deliver -- the EQ is empty. Branch 2 (the EQ was
not re-armed) is EXCLUDED as the cause of silence: a lost re-arm
would still show device-written EQEs once events occurred; the EQ
is empty for the entire silence while the device receives and
discards 60M+ packets. The remaining signature -- an armed EQ, an
armed CQ with pending owned completions, no device-written EQE and
no IRQ, self-recovering when it recovers -- is the memo's
device/firmware row.

## Honest caveats

- eq_update_ci is a static inline writing __raw_writel to the
  doorbell MMIO; the device-side commit of the last re-arm is not
  host-readable (recorded in the spec per the memo's fallback).
  The branch-3 call rests on the state facts above, which are
  complete host-side for rows 1 and 3.
- T1A-1/2 are FULL-WEDGE cells (the silence spans 200+ s
  continuously; the mid-cell ~1 s gaps are the substructure);
  T1A-3..6 trickled with wake-flowing gaps only. The state facts
  are identical in every gap of both regimes.
- The wake-flowing gaps show the same empty-EQ/no-IRQ state; their
  wakeups (trace-side) come from outside the completion path.
- The bpftrace E/I timestamps are relative (the sign-wrap artifact
  from the DR-012 note); the WALL/BEGIN anchors join them to the
  probe's wall clock (probe.meta wall_t0).
