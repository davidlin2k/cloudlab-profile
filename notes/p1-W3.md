## Pre-registered verdicts (spec v1-v3; numbers frozen before the runs)

- **P1 (knee prediction): FAILS.** Predicted F_P0 62.0k (bar 46.5-77.5k),
  F_P0X 82.4k (bar 61.8-103.0k); measured 190k and 260k (3.06x and 3.16x).
  The low-load per-CPU cost model does not transfer to TCP: the per-request
  cost falls with load (P0 16.1 us at 20k -> 5.5 us at 130k -> 3.3 us at
  300k; AN-008). Per the spec's outcome clause the model's claim scope
  shrinks: the Fig. 4 claim stands for UDP, not TCP. (C-017 Refuted.)
- **P2 (kill criterion): FIRES AGAINST the collapse claim.** At 2.0x the
  knee (380k) delivered goodput is 320,540 (P0) and 369,457 (P0X), i.e.
  169% and 194% of the knee goodput -- far above the 90% plateau rule.
  The UDP collapse does NOT survive TCP: flow control holds delivery at a
  capacity plateau (P0 ~320k, P0X ~370k). Transport-independence is
  Refuted (C-016). What survives TCP is the SLO/latency degradation and
  the co-location cost, not the collapse.
- **P3 (>= 5x wake-delay at the knee): FAILS.** p99(P0)/p99(P0X) at 190k =
  846/585 = 1.45x. (C-018 Refuted; the sched-pair extraction will place the
  wake-delay magnitude in the TCP path, mechanism not guessed.)
- **P4 (SLO direction): FAILS as stated.** Both arms breach the 1.05 ms SLO
  at 0.25x knee (p99 1267 / 1147 us; low-load latency anomaly, AN-008
  family). The co-location difference shows from 1.5x knee up: P0X holds
  SLO frac 0.999-1.000 through 285k while P0 falls to 0.829; at 380k
  0.935 vs 0.799.
