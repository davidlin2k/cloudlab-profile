# AN-004: launch-delayed senders truncate sender logs and deflate budgets

Date: 2026-09-24 (corrected same day; first draft wrongly attributed the
class to wall-clock steps -- k2_rx's now_s() is CLOCK_MONOTONIC and the
consumer never exits early; the draft's mechanism is struck) ·
Class: instrument · Found: wedge A/B (wedge-m0/m1), fig1-3b/c cells

## Signature
A cell's sender logs show k5blast status lines ending at wall=20-24 s
with no final "[k5blast] dip=" summary in some of the five logs
(snd_full < 5). The cell itself runs its full span (consumer summary
complete). Rows from these cells show sent below the nominal budget:
internally consistent delivery fractions, but the offered-rate axis is
wrong (part of the window ran 3/5 or 4/5 senders).

## Mechanism (established)
Sender-launch ssh bursts get throttled (the cloudlab-testbed skill's
known class): a sender whose launch lands late (or lands at all) starts
its 71 s budget late, is still sending at the cell's end, and the NEXT
cell's start-kill truncates its log mid-status -- no summary line.
20 of 24 wedge-A/B cells show the class.

## Affected runs
Any cell with snd_full < 5 (20/24 wedge-m0/m1; some fig1-3b/c cells).
Action: EXCLUDE from figure curves (analyzer's snd_full column; the
figures and fig4 filters require 5); the wedge-classification verdicts
of AN-003 remain valid for these cells (delivery-fraction endpoints are
comparable; the wedge is denominator-independent).

## Guard that now catches it
p1_analyze.py emits snd_full (sender summaries present, 5 = every
budget's full span observed); analysis/p1_figures.py and p1_fig4.py
refuse rows with snd_full != 5. Remaining hardening queued: retry sender
launches in p1cell (the fetch already retries; the launch does not).
