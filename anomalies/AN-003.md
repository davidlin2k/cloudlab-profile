## What broke
With threaded NAPI enabled (P2/P3/P4) and a UDP flood at or above ~525 kpps
on queue 7, the queue's NAPI processing can stop COMPLETELY and permanently
for the rest of the run: the napi kthread stops receiving wakeups while the
NAPI instance stays marked scheduled, its MSI-X IRQ (312) stays masked and
frozen, and the NIC ring overflows at roughly the wire rate
(rx_out_of_buffer grows ~750k/s) while the consumer receives a trickle.

## How it was detected
fig1-3 matrix rows for P3/P4 at 790k showed goodput 0-40 pps against a
56M-packet offer. Per-second p1samp data (cpu.log) for W1-P3-r790000-p64
shows the tracked queue-7 napi thread (cumulative run-time 187s -> 590s ->
726s across the batch, identity certain) with run-time delta exactly 0.000
and wait-time delta 0 across consecutive samples, irqcount frozen at
241079929, while rx_out_of_buffer grew. A live-state probe (p1wedge.sh)
confirmed: thread state S, 0 utime/stime, IRQ counts frozen from t=4s on,
oob growing ~750k/s. Sender-side counters clean throughout: exact paced
budgets, enobufs=0, tx_queue_dropped=0, tx_discards_phy=0, zero pause at
either end. The wire is fine; the receiver's ring is never drained.

## Mechanism (established vs open)
ESTABLISHED: NAPI marked scheduled -> IRQ masked -> kthread never woken ->
ring overflow forever. The trickle that arrives matches occasional spurious
wakeups. Remote placement (thread on cpu 40 or 9) wedged in 7/8 matrix runs
at >=790k; same-core threaded (P2, cpu 8) was immune 3/3 in the matrix.
OPEN: the trigger is state-sensitive. The p1wedge chain at 790k produced
the OPPOSITE pattern (P4 82% delivered at 720k/s p50 12.5us, P3 2%, P2 0
packets) and the matrix's P3-525k rep3 wedged while rep1/rep2 did not.
Candidate conditions under test: C-state pinning (/dev/cpu_dma_latency is
held open in p1cell but was NOT in p1wedge), threaded=0/1 cycling history,
NAPI kthread affinity history (one probe showed affinity 0-63 after p1pol's
taskset -pc 40 - either the pin failed silently or the affinity is reset).
