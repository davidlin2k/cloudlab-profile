## What broke
With threaded NAPI enabled (P2/P3/P4) and a UDP flood at or above ~525 kpps
on queue 7, the queue's NAPI processing can stop COMPLETELY and permanently
for the rest of the run: the napi kthread stops receiving wakeups while the
NAPI instance stays marked scheduled, its MSI-X IRQ (312) stays masked and
frozen, and the NIC ring overflows at roughly the wire rate
(rx_out_of_buffer grows ~750k/s) while the consumer receives a trickle.

## How it was detected
fig1-3 matrix rows for P3/P4 at 790k showed goodput 0-40 pps against a
56M-packet offer. Per-second p1samp data (cpu.log) for W1-P3-r790000-p64
shows the tracked queue-7 napi thread (cumulative run-time 187s -> 590s ->
726s across the batch, identity certain) with run-time delta exactly 0.000
and wait-time delta 0 across consecutive samples, irqcount frozen at
241079929, while rx_out_of_buffer grew. A live-state probe (p1wedge.sh)
confirmed: thread state S, 0 utime/stime, IRQ counts frozen from t=4s on,
oob growing ~750k/s. Sender-side counters clean throughout: exact paced
budgets, enobufs=0, tx_queue_dropped=0, tx_discards_phy=0, zero pause at
either end. The wire is fine; the receiver's ring is never drained.

## Mechanism (established vs open)
ESTABLISHED: NAPI marked scheduled -> IRQ masked -> kthread never woken ->
ring overflow forever. The trickle that arrives matches occasional spurious
wakeups. Remote placement (thread on cpu 40 or 9) wedged in 7/8 matrix runs
at >=790k; same-core threaded (P2, cpu 8) was immune 3/3 in the matrix.
OPEN: the trigger is state-sensitive. The p1wedge chain at 790k produced
the OPPOSITE pattern (P4 82% delivered at 720k/s p50 12.5us, P3 2%, P2 0
packets) and the matrix's P3-525k rep3 wedged while rep1/rep2 did not.
Candidate conditions under test: C-state pinning (/dev/cpu_dma_latency is
held open in p1cell but was NOT in p1wedge), threaded=0/1 cycling history,
NAPI kthread affinity history (one probe showed affinity 0-63 after p1pol's
taskset -pc 40 - either the pin failed silently or the affinity is reset).

   ## Root-cause HYPOTHESIS (PI review, 2026-09-24 -- untested until the
   diagnostic passes; recorded so it cannot be explained away)
   Since 2017 mlx5's poll routine checks whether it is running on a CPU in
   the IRQ's affinity mask; if busy and not on such a CPU it completes
   NAPI and re-arms the completion queues so the next interrupt moves
   polling to the "right" CPU. That design assumes NAPI runs in softirq on
   the interrupt's own CPU. Threaded NAPI breaks the assumption (its
   kthread runs where pinned/scheduled), so every busy poll may bail and
   wait for an interrupt; a lost re-arm under a full ring leaves exactly
   this signature: IRQ masked, thread asleep, ring overflowing. Onset
   pattern fits (P2 2-3 s first -- its thread competes with the app and
   the scheduler can drift it off the IRQ CPU sooner; P4 up to 68 s), and
   one observed kthread affinity was silently reset to all CPUs.

   Planned tests (PI-designed, all 2026-09-24): (1) 1 Hz ethtool -S watch
   of ch7_(aff_change|arm|poll|eq_rearm) during threaded floods vs inline
   control -- aff_change climbing on P2/P3/P4 only is the interaction;
   (2) decisive A/B: P4 wiring with IRQ 312's mask widened to include the
   kthread's CPU -- wedge gone and aff_change ~0 confirms; (3) severity:
   stop the flood mid-wedge; a queue that stays dead = remotely
   triggerable DoS; (4) novelty: lore.kernel.org for mlx5 threaded-NAPI
   affinity fixes + newest mainline probe (a 2026-08 patch fixes threaded
   NAPI never YIELDING under load -- different symptom: it spins, ours
   sleeps). If confirmed the fix is small: skip mlx5's affinity check when
   NAPI is threaded -- an upstream patch and the paper's concrete instance
   of two independently designed loops breaking each other.
   Script: p1/wdiag.sh (v2: aligned/misaligned A/B with single-CPU masks,
unpinned severity arm, 195 s cells, effective+requested affinity
snapshots, aff_change positive control, devlink rx health in recovery).

## Counter evidence to date (2026-09-24, live watch)
ch7_aff_change = 22,884,345 vs ch8 = 3,982 and idle channels ~5k: the
affinity-bailout has fired tens of millions of times on exactly the
flooded queue. arm/poll = 271.9M/363.7M = 0.75: ~75% of polls end by
arming and waiting for an interrupt instead of repolling -- the idiom
degrading threaded polling into one interrupt per batch. This ALSO
explains the threaded rungs' pre-wedge underperformance (P3 35.9% at
1.5x knee even in windows that did not wedge). Causation for the wedge
itself is the A/B's to establish (aligned vs misaligned, 8 cells/arm,
195 s, survival reporting).
