# strand work; the wedges correlate with the unpinned/migrating thread

## What was tested (specs/p1-CAUSAL.md, frozen before any run)

RQ1 held the poll thread's location fixed off-mask (pinned to CPU 10,
verified per-sample by the readiness probe; IRQ 312 on CPU 8; stock
driver) and asked whether the stop-and-rearm completion path strands
ready work. The readiness probe (999 Hz, validated on healthy and
dead queues per the frozen precondition) sampled cc, the ownership
bit at the consumer index, rq/ch counters, the napi state byte, and
the thread's cpu.

## The result (counts in notes/p1-CAUSAL-1.md)

- **Arm A (pinned off-mask, as-is driver): 0/8 stalls; 0
  ready-unserved gaps; the pin held.** Every cell ran the 790k flood
  at full drain (PROBE-OK at the 300k ceiling).
- The frozen decision rule's negative branch fires: per DR-010, the
  stop-and-rearm mechanism at a fixed off-mask location is NOT the
  cause. RQ3 does not proceed; arm B (F1) was not built (no delta to
  measure).
- The wedge DID reproduce -- but only in cells where the thread was
  NOT pinned: val-2 (migrating 45<->46, 2 owned-gaps, 18.4
  ready-unserved sample-s) and val-3 (6 owned-gaps, 85.9 s), plus
  PERSIST's unpinned A1 (2/10) and A7 (1/10) arms. E2's in-mask pin8
  cells were 0/8.

## What this record establishes (facts only)

1. The detour firing at a FIXED off-mask location is not sufficient
   to stall the queue (0/8).
2. Ready-but-unserved work during the stalls is real and now
   instrument-verified (the val-2/val-3 gaps; the +64 budget-step
   signature in the raw csv).
3. The stall condition, across every wedged cell on this node to
   date, is the UNPINNED/migrating thread under flood -- not the
   fixed off-mask location, not the in-mask location.
4. The instrument (rq1_probe.py + find_napi_thread.py + the
   sched_setaffinity pin) is validated and reusable; its three
   build lessons are committed (pointer-deref stats, masked frag
   index, correlation-based thread identification).
