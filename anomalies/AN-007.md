## Addendum 2026-09-25 04:0xZ -- the arbiter and the verdict

perf stat -a -C 8 -e cycles over a live P0X 390k cell: 67,656,675,321
cycles / 60 s. TSC calibration: 3,256,316,473 ref-cycles in 1.0019 s
busy loop -> TSC = 3.250 GHz. Full re-run with the fixed instrument
(per-CPU cycles+ref-cycles over the measure window):

| policy | rate | cpu8 busy PMU (s/63s) | cpu8 busy /proc/stat (s/60s) | ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0X | 390k | 36.53 | 0.22 | 166x |
| P0 | 390k | 57.43 | 42.07 | 1.4x |
| P0 | 130k | 19.28 | 11.59 | 1.7x |

Kernel facts: CONFIG_IRQ_TIME_ACCOUNTING is not set (HAVE=y, off),
NO_HZ_FULL=y, HZ=1000. Sub-tick IRQ-context bursts on an idle CPU are
charged to the idle bucket; ticks land inside bursts only rarely, so
the irq and softirq columns miss the work. When a thread keeps the CPU
busy (P0) the columns capture most of it; on a pure interrupt CPU
(P0X) they capture ~0.6%.

Verdict: C-014 CONFIRMED (the metric excludes softirq executed in
interrupt context; on this kernel the work is charged to idle, outside
every busy field). The corrected metric = ref-cycles / TSC per CPU,
bracketed to the consumer's mpkts window; the /proc/stat sum is kept as
cpu8_busy_stat_s (the gap measure). Also fixed in the same pass:
p1_analyze's softirq_s read steal (d[7]) not softirq (d[6]) -- the
source of the cal-1 anchor note's phantom "hidden share"; and the
first PMU bracket spanned 73 s against the consumer's 63 s window
(16% cost overcount) -- the task1b re-run uses the aligned bracket.
