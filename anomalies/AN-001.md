# AN-001: Emulator timer dropped scheduler ticks silently under load
Found: 2026-09-23, Hermes · Severity: invalidates results

## What happened
Both emitter generations lost scheduling ticks without any counter
seeing it. Generation 2 fed all streams from one shared
time.Ticker (cap-1 channel): when a striped writer blocked, the tick
was dropped and the overrun gate saw nothing. Generation 3 moved to
per-stream queues but kept a 1ms ticker whose cap-1 channel dropped
ticks at 12.5k+ goroutines per process: at 150k streams the wheel
ran 29.0 steps/s against the intended 40.0, and per-stream delivery
tracked the degraded step rate exactly (29.0 tok/s per stream). The
emitters reported zero drops and near-zero CPU throughout.

## How it was detected
A direct counter probe over 10s at 150k: steps advanced +290
(29.0/s) where the wheel must advance +400 (40.0/s), and delivery
per stream equalled the step rate to three digits. The probe came
from the unified-emitter gate design (spec C: gate on unintended
skips, log backpressure as a signal) — the counters existed but the
drop path was unaccounted.

## What it touches
- Runs: every pre-fix sweep (v2 kit: staircase 10k-200k, all phases)
  and the A/B per-hop and bypass cells run on the v3 ticker
  (50k/150k, proxy and bypass) → action: supersede
- Readings: the "1.5M tok/s plateau" (v2), the "~1.85M endpoint
  ceiling" (v3 ticker), and the retracted H2 1.75x alignment effect
  → action: supersede
- Not touched: the emission-shape validation (vLLM 1 segment per
  token), the wire-level smear measurement (burst_analyze on NIC
  captures), and the HAProxy per-token CPU accounting (pidstat).

## Fix and guard
schedule() now runs on absolute deadlines (sleep to t0 + k·1ms; a
late pass runs late and is counted, never skipped). Verified: steps
advance exactly 40.0/s under load. Guard: every sample records the
steps counter beside wall time (steps/s must equal 1/period) and
slot_overruns counts lateness >500us as a visible signal.
