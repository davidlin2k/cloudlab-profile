## The day's pitfall (recorded so it is never repeated)

The probe's hand-derived `vsi->rx_rings` offset (3312, from the 6.18.9
sources) points at **tx_rings** on 6.17.8; rx_rings is at **+3304**.
Every earlier contradiction -- pkts frozen at 4-5 (that was
tx-7.packets), ntc idle, rx_bi pages NULL, descriptors "changing"
(they were TX descriptors being reused) -- was this one off-by-8.
Verified against ethtool per-queue counters as ground truth; all ring
fields (ntp@128, ntu@138, ntc@140, stats@152, size@224) are correct.

Also: two identical-looking "Permission denied" wedges were (a) the
regenerated root key not yet on the sender and (b) running the flood
from a separate tool call after the probe had exited (the r6615
same-trap lesson: no cross-call timing).
